### About Me

I'm part of a team at Walmart Global Tech that engineers the backend
handling **300,000 to 500,000 orders a day** for Walmart's store
fulfillment systems. My background started in Electrical and Electronics
Engineering (College of Engineering, Trivandrum), and I've spent the last
7+ years moving from application development into software engineering
and DevOps/SRE work — Python automation, observability tooling, and
microservices at scale.

I'm currently doing an MTech in Artificial Intelligence at BITS Pilani
(specializing in Deep Learning and LLMs), extending that scale-and-
reliability background into the ML side of things.

- Building and operating microservices that keep order fulfillment
  running reliably at Walmart's scale.
- Deep in observability: Splunk, Dynatrace, Grafana, Prometheus, Kibana —
  monitoring, alerting design, and RCA on production incidents.
- Google Cloud certified across architecture, ML, DevOps, security,
  networking, and development.
